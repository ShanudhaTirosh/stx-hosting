## How paying works

Checkout creates an **invoice** and shows the payment methods we accept for your currency, with the account details. Pay the **exact invoice total**, then upload your receipt on the same page (or from **Billing**).

Receipts can be a PNG, JPEG, WEBP or PDF, up to 5 MB. Each receipt can be used once: uploading the same file for another invoice is refused.

## What the statuses mean

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Unpaid | The invoice is open. Pay and upload your receipt. |
| Payment pending | We have your receipt and a person is checking it. |
| Paid | Approved. Your service is unlocked or being prepared. |

You get an email when we receive your receipt, when it is approved, and when it is rejected.

## If a receipt is rejected

The email tells you why, for example the amount did not match. The invoice stays open: upload a corrected receipt and we look again. You can send up to five receipts for one invoice. If you are stuck, use **Support**.

## Invoices

Each invoice has a printable page. When PDF invoices are enabled, the email carries the PDF and you can open it from the invoice page. Invoices are only visible to your team.

## Cards and refunds

We do not take card payments. Refunds follow the Refund policy; ask through **Support**.

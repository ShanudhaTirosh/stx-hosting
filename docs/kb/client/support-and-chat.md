Open **Support** and start a conversation with a subject and a message. Everyone on your team can see your team's conversations, and our replies appear in the same place.

## Attachments

You can attach up to 3 files per message, 5 MB each: PNG, JPEG, WEBP, PDF or plain text. We check the real file type, so a renamed file is refused. Only your team and our support staff can open them.

## Replies

If we reply while you are not looking at the conversation, you get an email (at most one every ten minutes per conversation). Open the link to read and answer.

## Tips

- Say what you did, what you expected and what happened. Add the invoice number or server name.
- Screenshots of errors help a lot.
- You can also use the contact form or our Discord.

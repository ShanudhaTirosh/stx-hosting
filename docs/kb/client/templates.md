Templates are ready-made applications curated and verified by our engineering team, including CMS platforms, publishing suites, workflow automations, and developer tools.

## Available Templates

Our curated catalog includes popular self-hosted applications such as:
- **Supabase** (Full self-hosted BaaS: Kong, Auth, PostgREST, Realtime, Storage, Studio)
- **WordPress** (CMS & blogging)
- **Ghost** (Modern newsletter and publication platform)
- **n8n** (Visual workflow automation with PostgreSQL backend)
- **Nextcloud** (Self-hosted cloud collaboration and storage)
- **Strapi** (Headless CMS for building custom APIs)
- **Plausible** (Privacy-friendly analytics)
- **Uptime Kuma** (Uptime monitoring and status alerts)

## How to Deploy

1. Navigate to **Deploy Application** in the customer portal (`/portal/deploy`).
2. On the **1-Click Templates** tab, browse or filter templates by category.
3. Click your desired template to inspect its memory and CPU requirements.
4. Set an **Application Name** (e.g. `my-company-blog`). An instant preview of your live HTTPS address is displayed.
5. Optionally configure custom environment variables (defaults are pre-configured).
6. Click **Deploy Application**.

Your app container starts immediately with a dedicated wildcard HTTPS address, pre-configured ports, and automatic restart policies. If you need a template that is not in the catalog, reach out via **Support**.

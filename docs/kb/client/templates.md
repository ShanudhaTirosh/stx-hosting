---
title: One-click templates
summary: Start from ready-made apps and databases instead of your own code.
order: 4
---
Templates are ready-made setups chosen by us, such as popular apps and databases.

1. Open **Projects**, create or open a project, and add a **new resource**.
2. Pick **Templates** (or the database you want) and choose one.
3. Review the settings, then deploy.

Templates need an active plan of the matching kind: app hosting for apps, and a database plan for databases. Each template shows the resources it needs. If a template you expect is missing, ask through **Support**.

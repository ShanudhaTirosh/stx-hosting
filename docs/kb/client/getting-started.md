## The short version

1. **Choose a plan.** Browse the plans, pick a billing term, and add it to your cart.
2. **Sign in or create an account.** Your cart is kept while you do.
3. **Place the order.** We create an invoice. You are not charged yet.
4. **Pay.** Use one of the payment methods shown on the invoice, for example a bank transfer, and keep the receipt.
5. **Upload your receipt.** A person checks it and emails you at each step.
6. **Your service goes live.** Apps, bots, websites and databases start as soon as the payment is approved. A VPS is set up by hand, usually within **24 to 72 hours**, and we email you when it is ready.

## Where things are

- **My Services**: your servers and plans, with login details.
- **Deploy Wizard**: 1-click launch of Git apps, Docker containers, and curated application templates (`/portal/deploy`).
- **Mail**: business email hosting, custom domains, mailboxes, aliases, and direct webmail access (`/portal/mail`).
- **Billing**: invoices, receipts and their status.
- **Domains**: use your own domain name on an app with automated HTTPS.
- **Backups**: schedule, download, and multi-target restore database and app volume backups.
- **Support**: talk to our team via real-time live chat.
- **Help Center**: these guides.

## Good to know

- Prices are in USD or LKR. Use the currency switch at the top of the store.
- You can only manage servers we provide. You cannot add or import a server from another provider.
- When the period you paid for ends, new deployments stop. To continue, order the plan again.
